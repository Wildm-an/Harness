# Slide design

Read this before you make a new deck. A deck must look planned: the same colors, fonts, margins, and positions on each slide.

## Steps

1. Find the subject, the audience, and the tone (formal, friendly, technical, bold).
2. Select one palette from the table, or make one from the brand colors of the user.
3. Select one font pair.
4. Make a grid: the margins, the title position, and the content area. Use the same grid on each slide.
5. Select a layout for each slide type. Use at least three different layouts in a deck of more than five slides.

## Palettes

Each palette has a dark color (text or dark backgrounds), a light color (backgrounds), a muted color (secondary text), and one or two accent colors. Use the accent for one thing on each slide: a key number, a highlight, a chart series.

Rules:

- Text on a light background uses the dark color. Text on a dark background uses white or the light color.
- The contrast between text and background must be high. Do not put muted text on a colored background.
- Use a dark background for the title and section slides, and a light background for content slides. Or use light backgrounds for all slides. Do not mix more than two background colors.

Use fonts that are on the computer of the audience. A missing font changes the layout.

## Sizes (16:9, 13.333 x 7.5 in)

| Element | Size |
|---|---|
| Title of the deck | 40 to 54 pt |
| Slide title | 28 to 36 pt |
| Body text and bullets | 16 to 24 pt. Never less than 14 pt. |
| Key number | 48 to 72 pt |
| Captions, sources | 11 to 12 pt |
| Margins | 0.5 to 0.7 in on each side |
| Space between blocks | 0.3 to 0.5 in |

## Layouts

| Slide type | Layout |
|---|---|
| Title | Dark background. Large title at the left or center, subtitle under it. |
| Section | Dark or accent background. Section number and title. |
| Bullets | Title at the top. 3 to 5 bullets at the left, a picture, icon, or chart at the right. |
| Key number | One large number in the accent color, with one line that explains it. |
| Two columns | Two blocks of the same width, each with a short heading. Use it for before and after, or for two options. |
| Three cards | Three rectangles of the same size, each with a heading and two lines of text. |
| Chart | Title that states the finding ("Sales grew 12%"), not the topic ("Sales"). Chart below or at the right. |
| Table | At most 6 rows and 5 columns. Bold header row with a fill. |
| Timeline | A line with 3 to 6 points, each with a date and a short label. |
| Quote | Large quote text, the name of the person below it. |
| Closing | Summary of 2 to 3 points, or the next steps, or the contact details. |

## Content rules

- One message for each slide. The title states the message.
- At most 6 bullets on a slide, at most about 12 words for each bullet.
- Give each slide a visual element: a chart, a picture, an icon, a shape, or a large number. A slide with only text is weak.
- Use the same position for the title on each slide.
- Align the shapes to the grid. Make the edges of shapes line up.
- Do not use a line under the title. Use space and size to separate the title.
- Do not use more than one accent color on a slide.
